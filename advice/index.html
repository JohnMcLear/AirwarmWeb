Practical heat pump guidance, in plain English

We are writing a set of guides covering the questions that actually come up — suitability, heat loss, radiators, hot water, controls and running costs. They are listed below so you can see what is coming.

Nothing here is published yet

These guides are in preparation. We have chosen not to put up thin, half-written articles in the meantime: heat pump advice is worth doing properly or not at all. If you have a question now, e-mail us and you will get a straight answer from a person.
