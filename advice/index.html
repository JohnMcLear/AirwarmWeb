Practical heat pump guidance, in plain English

Eleven guides covering the questions that actually come up: suitability, heat loss, radiators, hot water, controls, running costs, funding, renovations, servicing and faults. No sales material, no predicted savings, and no pretending every property is straightforward.

Most homes can be heated well by an air source heat pump. Not every home can be today, and not every home without changes first — so where there is a constraint, these guides name it and explain what would resolve it. If you would rather just ask a person, e-mail us and you will get a straight answer.
