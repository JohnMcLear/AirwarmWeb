What the Home Energy Assessment is, and is not

This matters more than anything else on this page, so it is stated plainly. The Home Energy Assessment is a short questionnaire that gives you an indicative sense of whether a heat pump is worth exploring for your property, based only on what you tell it.

It is not:

  • a heat loss calculation;
  • a technical design or specification;
  • a survey;
  • a quotation, an offer or a price indication; or
  • a final answer about your property.

The outcome is produced by software from your answers, and is then read by a person before we act on it. A person can and does disagree with it in both directions. Real suitability is determined by a survey, carried out by someone who has been to the property, and we explain our reasoning to you. Please do not treat an on-screen outcome as a commitment by us to do the work, or as a reason to commit money elsewhere.
