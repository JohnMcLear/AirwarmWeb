Why Airwarm is being built this way round

Homeowners are routinely asked to make expensive heating decisions using inconsistent advice. Airwarm exists to reduce that uncertainty: to translate technical requirements into clear choices, design around the property rather than the product, and stay available after the installation is finished.

Where Airwarm is today

Airwarm is a new business, and we would rather say so than pretend otherwise. AIRWARM LTD is registered in England and Wales, company number 17362672, with its registered office in Baildon — you can look all of that up rather than take our word for it, and the details are in the footer of every page. The process, the survey method and the aftercare commitment described on this site are written down and being put in place before we take on work.

What we do not have yet is a trading history. There is no installation count, no star rating, no review and no accreditation claim on this site, because the company was only just incorporated. Reviews, photographs of completed installations and certification details will appear here when they are real and can be checked — not before. When you get in touch we will tell you plainly what we can and cannot do yet.

Thomas Robinson, Founder & Director

Airwarm is being set up by Thomas Robinson, who is responsible for its technical standards, the way it is run and the quality of the work it puts its name to. He is the director of AIRWARM LTD, which is registered in England and Wales.

The intention is a company that outlasts its founder rather than a job with a logo on it: defined roles, written procedures, proper training and a management system, so the standard does not depend on one person being in the van.

That means the founder’s job is to set the standard, build the system and develop the people who can carry it — not to become the permanent bottleneck for every survey approval, purchase order and customer update.

Photograph to come: a genuine photograph of Thomas Robinson. The brand pack is explicit that a real founder photograph builds more trust than an anonymous stock model, so this space stays empty until there is a real one.

How decisions get made

A decision is only acceptable at Airwarm if it protects safety, technical quality, customer trust, the cash position and long-term reputation. Where those pull against each other, safety and honesty come first. A sale never overrides suitability or engineering judgement.

What that looks like in practice

  • Turning down work that will not perform
  • Saying “insulate first” when that is the honest answer
  • Showing the assumptions behind a calculation
  • Writing verbal promises down
  • Naming what is excluded, not only what is included

Why Airwarm exists

The problem it is answering

Heat pumps have a reputation problem in this country, and most of it is deserved by the installations rather than by the technology. A unit sized from a rule of thumb, radiators left as they were, controls on factory defaults and nobody available afterwards will produce a house that is expensive to heat and an owner who tells their neighbours so.

Almost none of that is a heat pump problem. It is a design and aftercare problem, and it is fixable by doing ordinary things carefully and writing them down.

What it is trying to be

The ambition is to become one of Yorkshire’s most trusted renewable-heating companies, known for careful design, respectful installation and long-term customer support. That is an ambition rather than a description, and it will be earned one installation at a time.

The mission underneath it is narrower and more practical: help homeowners make confident energy decisions by assessing homes honestly, designing appropriate low-temperature heating systems, installing them properly and supporting how they perform over time.

The customer Airwarm is built for

Someone who values correct design, is willing to consider the emitter or fabric work that a calculation actually justifies, understands that the home has to be assessed before anything is promised, and wants an installer who is still accountable after commissioning. If that is you, we will get on well.

What we are committing to

Five words that are easy to print and harder to keep. They are here so you can measure us against them.

Integrity

Recommending the correct solution even when it is the less profitable one. That includes telling people when a heat pump is not their best next step, and saying it before a survey fee rather than after one.

Technical care

Checking assumptions rather than inheriting them, documenting decisions so they can be reviewed, and continuing to train. Every design is reviewed by someone other than the person who produced it before the proposal is issued.

Respect

Protecting the home, communicating clearly, arriving when we said we would and arriving prepared. The standard applies to the telephone manner and the paperwork as much as to the pipework.

Accountability

Owning outcomes and correcting problems, and treating every complaint as something that should improve the process rather than something to defend. Defensive communication is explicitly prohibited.

Improvement

Turning every project and every complaint into a better process. Completed jobs are sampled for design accuracy, installation finish, documentation, whether the customer actually understood the handover, and what we would do differently.

And one thing we will not do

Compete primarily on being the cheapest. Airwarm would rather compete on the result, and tell you where a cheaper approach would be a false economy and where it genuinely would not.

The Airwarm standard

The standard is not a poster in an office. It applies to every point at which you deal with us, and it is deliberately written to include the unglamorous ones — because those are the ones customers actually notice.

If any of these slips, we would rather hear about it than not.

How the company is being built

Documented systems first

Survey, design review, installation control, commissioning and aftercare are written procedures before they are habits. That is what makes a standard survive growth.

Training, not assumption

Manufacturer and technical training is being completed rather than claimed. Credentials will appear on this site once they are held and current, and not a day earlier.

Qualified contractors where they are needed

Electrical work is carried out by appropriately qualified contractors where required, working to Airwarm’s design and coordination. Subcontracting does not move Airwarm’s responsibility to you onto somebody else.

The people who install take part in the survey

Where practical, whoever would do the work is involved in or reviews the survey. It removes the gap between what was promised and what turns out to be possible.

Aftercare as a service, not a favour

48-hour contact, a two-week comfort review, seasonal optimisation where required and an annual service reminder. It protects your outcome and it tells us what to design better next time.

Limits stated out loud

No 24-hour or emergency response. No work outside our competence. No properties outside the service area. No projects where a genuinely necessary upgrade is refused.

Starting small, on purpose

Airwarm is starting with Bradford, Leeds and Shipley — an area small enough to serve properly, learn from and get right. Neighbouring West Yorkshire towns will be added when there is genuine coverage to offer, not before.

For the same reason the early service range is deliberately narrow: suitability reviews, surveys, design, installation, emitter upgrades, controls, commissioning and aftercare. Servicing, optimisation visits and solar or battery coordination come next.

It is also a genuinely interesting area to learn in. Solid stone terraces, back-to-backs, cellars, steep valley sides, exposed tops and a World Heritage Site are all within a few miles of each other, and between them they cover most of the hard problems in domestic retrofit.

Frequently asked questions

Is Airwarm MCS certified?

Airwarm’s certification route is being confirmed ahead of its trading launch, and it will be stated plainly and verifiably on this site once settled rather than claimed early. Any installer’s certification can be checked on the certification body’s public register, and you should check.

Why is Airwarm not the cheapest option?

Airwarm’s approach involves a proper heat loss calculation, a documented design, a design review before the proposal is issued and recorded commissioning. That work costs something. Airwarm would rather compete on the result than on being the lowest number, and will tell you where a cheaper approach would be a false economy and where it genuinely would not.

Who does the electrical work?

A heat pump is a substantial electrical load, so the incoming supply, main fuse and consumer unit are all checked at survey. Electrical work is carried out by vetted regional electrical contractors working to Airwarm’s design and coordination, and where the network operator needs to be involved Airwarm will say so early.

Airwarm has not traded yet. Why would I use it?

That is a fair question and it deserves a straight answer. You should not use Airwarm because of a claim on a website. Judge it on the things that exist now: a documented process you can ask to see, a suitability-first approach that is happy to turn work down, calculations shown with their assumptions, and a named person responsible for the standard. If that is not enough for a decision of this size, that is a reasonable conclusion to reach.

Does Airwarm offer emergency call-out?

No. Airwarm does not offer a 24-hour or emergency response service, and will not imply otherwise. Response expectations are stated plainly, and if you need cover of that kind it is better to know now.
