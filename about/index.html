Why Airwarm is being built this way round

Homeowners are routinely asked to make expensive heating decisions using inconsistent advice. Airwarm exists to reduce that uncertainty: to translate technical requirements into clear choices, design around the property rather than the product, and stay available after the installation is finished.

Where Airwarm is today

Airwarm is a new business, and we would rather say so than pretend otherwise. The process, the survey method and the aftercare commitment described on this site are written down and being put in place before we take on work. Reviews, photographs of completed installations and accreditation details will appear here when they are real and can be checked — not before.

Thomas Robinson, Founder & Director

Airwarm is being set up by Thomas Robinson, who is responsible for its technical standards, the way it is run and the quality of the work it puts its name to.

The intention is a company that outlasts its founder rather than a job with a logo on it: defined roles, written procedures, proper training and a management system, so the standard does not depend on one person being in the van.

How decisions get made

A decision is only acceptable at Airwarm if it protects safety, technical quality, customer trust, the cash position and long-term reputation. Where those pull against each other, safety and honesty come first. A sale never overrides suitability or engineering judgement.

What we are committing to

Integrity

Recommending the correct solution even when it is the less profitable one. That includes telling people when a heat pump is not their best next step.

Technical care

Checking assumptions rather than inheriting them, documenting decisions so they can be reviewed, and continuing to train.

Respect

Protecting the home, communicating clearly and arriving prepared. The standard applies to the telephone manner and the paperwork as much as to the pipework.

Accountability

Owning outcomes and correcting problems, and treating every complaint as something that should improve the process rather than something to defend.

Starting small, on purpose

Airwarm is starting with Bradford, Leeds and Shipley — an area small enough to serve properly, learn from and get right. Neighbouring West Yorkshire towns will be added when there is genuine coverage to offer, not before.

For the same reason the early service range is deliberately narrow: suitability reviews, surveys, design, installation, emitter upgrades, controls, commissioning and aftercare. Servicing, optimisation visits and solar or battery coordination come next.

Company and accreditation details: registration, VAT, insurance and certification details will be published here as they are confirmed. We would rather show you an empty space than a badge we have not earned yet.
