Cookie policy

This document is being prepared

Airwarm’s cookie policy has not been published yet. At the moment there is nothing for it to describe: this website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, which is also why you have not been asked to consent to anything.

We would rather show you an honest placeholder than generic wording lifted from another company’s website. If you have a question in the meantime, e-mail hello@airwarm.co.uk and a person will answer it.

What the website does today

It may help to know how little there is to disclose at this stage. This website sets no cookies, contains no analytics or tracking of any kind, and has no contact or enquiry form. The Home Energy Assessment runs entirely in your own browser: nothing you enter is sent to Airwarm, saved or shared. The only way to get in touch is to send an e-mail, which means you decide what you share and when.

The one exception worth naming: the Montserrat typeface is loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google to fetch it. That will be covered properly in the documents above once they are written.
