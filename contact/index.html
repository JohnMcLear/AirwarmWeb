What to include

You do not have to, but these five things let us give you a useful answer first time rather than asking a round of questions:

  • Roughly where the property is — town or postcode area is plenty
  • What kind of property it is, and roughly how old
  • How it is heated at the moment
  • What is prompting you to look at this now
  • Anything you already suspect might be a problem

About replies

Every e-mail is read and answered. Airwarm is not yet trading full-time, so we are not going to promise a response time we cannot guarantee — but if something is going to take a while, we will tell you that rather than leave you waiting.

What Airwarm does not offer

There is no 24-hour or emergency call-out service. If you have no heating and it is urgent, please contact an emergency heating engineer rather than waiting for us.

No contact form, on purpose

This site collects nothing. There is no contact form, no tracking and no cookies, because Airwarm has not yet chosen how it will hold customer information or published a privacy policy. Until it has, e-mail keeps you in control of what you share.
