What to include

You do not have to, but these five things let us give you a useful answer first time rather than asking a round of questions:

  • Roughly where the property is — town or postcode area is plenty
  • What kind of property it is, and roughly how old
  • How it is heated at the moment
  • What is prompting you to look at this now
  • Anything you already suspect might be a problem

Please do not send bank details, and there is no need to send photographs at this stage. If we need pictures later we will ask for specific ones.

What Airwarm cannot help with

Said plainly, because a quick no is more useful than a slow maybe.

Four things we will turn down

  • Emergencies. There is no 24-hour or emergency call-out service. If you have no heating and it is urgent, contact an emergency heating engineer rather than waiting for us.
  • Work outside our competence. If a system, make or fault is outside what Airwarm can properly support, you will be told rather than experimented on.
  • Properties outside the service area. Bradford, Leeds and Shipley at launch. Ask and you will get a straight answer about your postcode.
  • Projects where a genuinely necessary upgrade is refused. If the design needs a larger emitter and that is off the table, we would rather not install something that will disappoint you.

Frequently asked questions

Does Airwarm offer emergency call-out?

No. Airwarm does not offer a 24-hour or emergency response service, and will not imply otherwise. Response expectations are stated plainly, and if you need cover of that kind it is better to know now.

How much does a heat pump installation cost?

Airwarm is not publishing prices ahead of its trading launch, and a figure quoted without a survey would be a guess. What determines the cost is the size of the system, how many emitters need changing, the cylinder and its position, the pipework and electrical work required, and access. The proposal sets all of it out itemised.

Can Airwarm handle a grant application for me?

That depends on the certification route Airwarm holds at the time you enquire, and Airwarm will confirm its current position directly rather than implying a capability it does not yet hold.

How grant funding works, in principle

What if something goes wrong and I am unhappy?

Airwarm acknowledges promptly, establishes the facts, distinguishes a comfort expectation from a technical fault, assigns ownership, agrees the next action and documents the outcome. Defensive communication is not acceptable. A published complaints procedure sets out the route including escalation.

Why is there no telephone number?

Because there is not one yet. The business line has not been provisioned, and putting a mobile number on the site that nobody can answer during a survey would be worse than not having one. When there is a number it will appear here and in the footer at the same time.

Do you cover my area?

Launch coverage is Bradford, Leeds and Shipley, which includes Saltaire, Baildon, Windhill, Wrose, Frizinghall, Thackley, Idle and Nab Wood. Bingley, Ilkley, Keighley, Halifax, Huddersfield and Wakefield will be added only where there is genuine service coverage rather than a page on a website. If you are just outside, ask, and you will get an honest answer rather than a booking.
