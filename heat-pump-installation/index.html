An installation that is planned before it starts

Good installation is mostly preparation. By the time anyone arrives at your door, the design is settled, the equipment is on site and everybody knows what is happening on which day.

Before we start

Design confirmed and reviewed. Equipment and materials ordered and checked in. Any subcontracted work — electrical work, for example — arranged with vetted contractors. Access, parking, protection, pipe routes and your expectations all agreed in advance, along with a risk assessment for the work.

This is the part customers never see and the part that decides whether the week goes well.

Who this is for

  • Homeowners who have had a survey and accepted a proposal
  • Renovations where heating is one part of a larger programme
  • Properties needing emitter or hot-water work alongside the heat pump

Standards on site

Your home is protected

Floors and furniture covered, materials stored tidily, dust and noise controlled as far as the work allows, and the property left safe every evening.

You know what is happening

A daily update on what was done and what happens next. If something changes, you hear it from us on the day rather than finding out later.

The work is recorded

Concealed pipework photographed before it is covered, components labelled, and any change from the design written down with the reason.

Commissioning is a stage, not a formality

Flushing, filling, pressure testing, electrical confirmation, control setup, weather compensation, balancing, hot-water checks and a noise check. A system is not finished simply because it is producing heat.

Handover, and what happens after

You get a plain-English guide to your system: what the settings do, what normal behaviour looks like through the seasons, what to check before calling anyone, warranty information and the service schedule.

Then we stay in contact: a follow-up shortly after handover, a comfort review once you have lived with the system, seasonal optimisation where it is needed and an annual service reminder.

Airwarm does not offer a 24-hour emergency service. We will tell you what our actual response looks like rather than implying something we cannot deliver.

Included in every installation

  • Heat pump, cylinder and hydraulic arrangement installed to the design
  • Emitter upgrades where the design requires them
  • Controls and weather compensation configured, not left on defaults
  • Full commissioning with records
  • System guide and handover session
  • Warranty registration and service schedule
Photographs of finished work: tidy plant rooms, pipework and completed installations will be shown here once Airwarm has projects of its own to photograph. This site uses no stock imagery.

Installation questions

How long will my heating be off?

It depends on the scope. We plan the changeover deliberately and tell you before we start what to expect and on which days, including any temporary arrangements.

Will you make a mess?

Some disruption is unavoidable when pipework changes, but protection, tidy storage and daily clean-up are part of the standard rather than a favour.

Who does the electrical work?

Vetted, qualified electrical contractors where it falls outside our own scope. We coordinate it; you do not have to arrange it.

What if something goes wrong afterwards?

You contact us. Complaints are acknowledged promptly, the facts established, ownership assigned, the next action agreed with you and the outcome documented. Defensive communication is not acceptable to us.
