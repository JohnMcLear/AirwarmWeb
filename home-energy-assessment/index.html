Find out whether a heat pump is worth exploring for your home

Four short stages, a few minutes, no photographs and nothing to measure. You get an honest first view of where your property stands and what a sensible next step would be.

Stage 1 of 4

Your property

Just the first part, for example BD18 or LS6. We only use this to check whether you are in the area we cover.
An approximate answer is fine. “Not sure” is a perfectly good answer too.

Current condition

Best guesses are fine. We are looking for a general picture, not a survey.

How well insulated do you think the home is?
Is there space outside for the heat pump unit? It needs roughly the footprint of a large washing machine, against an outside wall, with clear air around it.
A heat pump heats hot water into a cylinder, so one is needed even if you currently have a combination boiler.
Is the property listed, in a conservation area, or leasehold? None of these rules a heat pump out. They change what is permitted, so it helps to know early.

Your plans

Would you consider changing some radiators if the design needed it? A heat pump runs at a lower temperature than a boiler, so some rooms often need a larger radiator. There is no wrong answer here — it just changes the advice.

Your result

What this is, and what it is not

This is an indicative first step, not a technical heat-loss survey. It does not produce a design, a specification or a price, and it is not a final answer — a person at Airwarm makes that judgement.

Your answers stay on your device

Stages one to three run entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to Airwarm, saved, or shared with anyone. There are no cookies and no analytics on this website. If you want to talk to us, you e-mail us — so you decide what to share and when.

No photographs needed

We do not ask for pictures at this stage. Nothing to measure, nothing to look up.

What the three results mean

Likely suitable

Several positive indicators. A short review call is worthwhile so we can confirm the main details and decide whether a full survey is the right next step.

Potentially suitable

Your property may be suitable, but one or two details need clarification. This is common, and we will explain what matters and what may need improving before a survey.

Not currently suitable

A heat pump may not be the best next step today. That is rarely permanent. We will explain the main constraints and suggest practical improvements or alternatives.
